What it is

A weekly meal planner for Indian kitchens.

The Meal Board is a board of 21 meals — three a day for seven days. You fill it with dishes, or load one of six ready-made weeks and change what you like.

Every one of the 96 full recipes scales to your household, lists what to soak or grind the night before, and opens into a step-by-step cooking mode. The board then writes your shopping list, added up across the week.

Beyond the recipes sits a library of 511 more dishes from every Indian state and the wider world, with the name in its own script, where it comes from, and links to watch it made.

Why use it

Less deciding. Better eating. One trip to the shop.

01

The 6 pm question, answered on Sunday

Deciding what to cook, three times a day, is the tiring part. Fill the week once — or load a ready one — and every meal after that is already decided.

02

One shopping list that adds itself up

The list builds from the board and totals the same ingredient across dishes. Sort it by vegetable shop, meat shop or kirana, and send it on WhatsApp.

03

Protein you can actually see

Every dish carries its protein per serving. The board shows each day as a bar, so a low day is obvious before you cook it, not after.

04

Cook without squinting at a phone

Cooking mode shows one step at a time in large type, with a timer ring on any step that has a time in it. It beeps, vibrates and keeps the screen awake.

05

Less waste, fewer pans

Cook double and tonight's dinner becomes tomorrow's tiffin. Leftovers fill the next slot on their own and are counted once on the list.

06

Your kitchen, your language, your phone

Six Indian languages, no account, no server. It works offline, and your weeks, ratings and notes never leave the device.

How it works

Plan once. Cook and shop from it all week.

1

Plan

Fill three meals a day for seven days, or load a ready week. Auto-fill works from your rules: diet, a weekday time limit, no repeats, a protein target.

2

Cook

Each recipe rescales to your household. Cooking mode shows one step at a time, with a timer on every step that has a time in it.

3

Shop

The list builds from the board, adding up the same ingredient across dishes. Sort it by shop, tick as you go, and copy it into WhatsApp.

Cooking mode

Hands full? The screen keeps up.

  • One step per screen, in type you can read from the stove.
  • A timer ring on any step with a time in it — tap to start, tap to pause. It beeps and vibrates when it is done.
  • Everything to get out first, scaled to how many you are cooking for.
  • The screen stays awake, and arrow keys move between steps on a computer.
